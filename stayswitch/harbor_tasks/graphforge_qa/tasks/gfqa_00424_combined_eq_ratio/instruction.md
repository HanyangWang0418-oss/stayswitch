The files pnc_bank_financials_fdic.json (PNC Bank) and bbva_usa_financials_fdic.json (BBVA USA) are FDIC financial extracts (dollar values in thousands). For the most recent quarter-end (REPDTE) that appears in both files, compute the combined equity-to-assets ratio of the two banks: (PNC EQ + BBVA EQ) divided by (PNC ASSET + BBVA ASSET), as a percentage rounded to 2 decimals.

The source files are in `/app/data/`. Work out the answer from them, then write ONLY the final answer
(a single number, no units or commentary; plain digits, e.g. 1234.56) to `/app/answer.txt`.
